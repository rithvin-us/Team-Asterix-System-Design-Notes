# Session II — Block Diagramming

> **Status:** stub — content to be added

## Topic

- Block diagramming
- Data flow
- Power distribution
- Physical linkages
- Diagramming conventions

## Objectives

By the end, a participant can:

- [ ] Draw a block diagram with a boundary, a legend and labelled arrows
- [ ] Distinguish data, power, control and mechanical flow visually
- [ ] Trace a data path end to end and find where it breaks
- [ ] Trace power to every consumer and spot the one with no supply
- [ ] Show physical linkages and load paths
- [ ] Keep all blocks at one level of zoom
- [ ] Save a diagram as `.drawio.svg`

## The one thing to drill

**Pick any arrow. Say what travels along it.**

Asked a hundred times, that single question is most of the skill. Everything else in
this session is support for it.

## After the session

Write up what came up — questions you could not answer, the explanation that landed
unexpectedly well, the misconception several people shared — into the relevant
[concept page](../02-concepts/concepts-index.md) the same day.

Things discovered while teaching are the most valuable notes here, and the easiest to
lose. They exist for about a day.
