# Session I. Subsystem Thinking

> **Status:** stub, content to be added

## Topic

- Subsystem thinking
- Breaking a complex ATV into inputs, processing units and outputs
- Thinking across mechanical, electrical and software domains at once

## Objectives

By the end, a participant can:

- [] Draw the boundary of a system and say what is outside it
- [] Break an ATV into subsystems, each with one stated responsibility
- [] Identify inputs, processing and outputs for each subsystem
- [] Explain why a given cut is better than an alternative cut
- [] Recognise when a "subsystem" is really two, or really a label for confusion
