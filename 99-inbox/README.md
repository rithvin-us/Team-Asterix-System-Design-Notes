# 99-inbox — Quarantine

> **Nothing in this folder is canonical.**
>
> Everything here is raw input: machine-generated text, half-formed notes, things
> captured in a hurry with nowhere to file them. It is **not verified** and **not
> anyone's considered understanding**.
>
> Everything *outside* this folder has been written or rewritten deliberately.

---

## Why this folder exists

To prevent one specific failure: a repository where AI-generated text and the
author's actual understanding are mixed together and indistinguishable, so that a
year later nobody — the author included — can tell which parts were thought through
and which were generated and skimmed.

That failure is quiet. Nothing looks wrong. The notes are fluent and mostly correct.
But the repository stops being a record of what someone understands and becomes a
record of what a model said, and the difference only surfaces when someone relies on
it.

One folder, loudly labelled, fixes it. Location carries the warning, so there is
nothing to maintain per file.

## What goes in here

| | |
|---|---|
| [`notebooklm-raw/`](notebooklm-raw/) | Raw NotebookLM output, and the source list |
| [`unfiled.md`](unfiled.md) | Anything captured with nowhere to put it yet |

## Getting out of here

**Rewrite it in your own words.** Not reformat, not lightly edit. Rewrite, from
understanding, into the right place in
[02-concepts](../02-concepts/) or wherever it belongs.

**The test:** if you cannot rewrite it without looking at it, you do not understand
it yet, and it is not ready to be promoted.

That test is the whole mechanism. Copy-pasting a correct explanation produces a
repository that is correct and useless, because the understanding never moved from
the source into you.

## Draining this folder

It should trend towards empty. Not be empty — it is a working inbox — but trend that
way.

If it grows permanently, one of two things is true: promotion is too much work, or
this is being used as storage rather than as an inbox. Both are worth fixing when
noticed. A permanently full inbox is just a second repository with worse rules.
