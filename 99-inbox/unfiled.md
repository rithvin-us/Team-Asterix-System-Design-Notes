# Unfiled

Anything with nowhere to go yet. Append to the bottom. Do not tidy. Do not decide
where it belongs.

File it later, in batches, when you can see the shape of several notes at once.

**The cost of capture must stay near zero.** If filing is required at capture time,
capture stops happening — and the notes lost that way are the good ones, because the
good ones arrive at inconvenient moments.

---

## What belongs here

- Rough notes
- Classroom observations
- Things discovered while teaching
- Mistakes and corrections
- An explanation that landed well and should be written up properly
- Questions you could not answer
- Concepts to research later
- Half-formed connections between two things

## Format

One `##` heading with the date. Then whatever you have — a sentence, a fragment, a
link, a photo of a whiteboard. Tags optional, nothing required.

```markdown
## 2026-10-04

Someone asked why power flow gets its own arrow style when it is "just another
connection". Best answer in the room: because you can forget a data connection and
the system reports an error, but forget a power connection and nothing happens at
all. Write this up properly in flow-types.md.

Still cannot answer cleanly: when is a shared bus a single interface vs several?
```
