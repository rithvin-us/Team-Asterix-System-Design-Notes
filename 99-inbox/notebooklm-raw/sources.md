## Why record this at all

Two reasons, both practical.

**Attribution.** When something gets promoted into a concept page, you may want to
point at where the idea came from. Without this table, that information exists only
inside NotebookLM, which is not part of this repository and will not be there
forever.

**Checking.** Machine output is verified against its source or it is not verified.
If the source is not recorded, it cannot be checked, which means nothing from the
notebook can ever be promoted honestly.
