Export

Paste into your project's design record, under a dated heading. Scoring the same design again in three weeks is how you see whether it improved.

A design that scores well is not automatically good — but one that scores badly is reliably incomplete, and incomplete is the failure that actually shows up when you build. Pair this with the written rubric.
