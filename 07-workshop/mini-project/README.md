# System Design Mini Project

> **Status:** stub — brief to be written

The main deliverable of the system design portion.

## Brief

To be written. When it is, it states:

- The system to be designed
- What must be delivered
- Constraints given to participants
- Deadline and submission method
- Whether it is individual or team

## What to deliver

Expected shape — confirm when the brief is written:

- A context diagram, an HLD, and at least one LLD, as `.drawio.svg`
- A design record covering boundary, requirements, assumptions, interfaces, flows and trade-offs
- The trade-off table, with rejected options named

Use the [project template](../../06-projects/_TEMPLATE/README.md) as the design
record. Sections 1–6 and 9 are the relevant ones; implementation and testing may not
apply.

## How it is graded

Against **[the rubric](../ai-prompts/design-rubric.md)**. Same checklist, no hidden
criteria. Self-check against it before submitting.

The two things that most separate a strong submission from a weak one:

1. **Every arrow has a stated content, with units and rate.** This is the single most
   common gap and the easiest to fix.
2. **Trade-offs name the rejected option and what the choice costs.** A decision with
   no named alternative was a default, and will be marked as one.

## Using AI on this

Allowed and encouraged, with the prompts in [ai-prompts](../ai-prompts/). They are
built so the AI interrogates you rather than designing for you.

What is not allowed is handing in a generated architecture. It is also, practically,
easy to spot: a generated design has no numbers tied to your actual constraints, and
falls apart on the first "why this and not that?" — which you will be asked.
