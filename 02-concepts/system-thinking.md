# System Thinking

> **Status:** stub

Seeing something as a set of parts with relationships, rather than as one thing or as
a pile of components.

- Why the interesting failures live at the interfaces, not inside the blocks

## Guiding questions

- What is this system *for*? What does it do that no single part does?
- Which parts affect each other in ways not visible from the diagram?
- Which problems here are part problems, and which are relationship problems?
