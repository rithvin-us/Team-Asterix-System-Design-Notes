# HLD vs LLD

> **Status:** stub

**They are the same five moves at two levels of zoom. Not two disciplines, and not
two folder trees.**

| | Shows | Blocks |
|---|---|---|
| **Context** | Your system as one box, plus what it talks to | 1 + 3–6 external |
| **HLD** | The major blocks inside, and what passes between them | 5–9 |
| **LLD** | One HLD block, opened up | 5–9 |

An LLD **states which HLD block it expands**, in its first line. Without that, a
reader cannot locate it in the system.

**Not every HLD block needs an LLD.** Write one when a block is genuinely complex,
contested, or about to be built. An LLD per block produces diagrams that restate
their parent with more rectangles.
