# Assumptions and Constraints

> **Status:** stub

Constraints box you in. Assumptions are things you believe but have not checked. They
are not the same, and mixing them is expensive.

An unstated assumption is indistinguishable from a mistake.

## To write up here

- Constraints: budget, parts available, rules, deadline, team skills, physical envelope
- Constraints as design input rather than as excuse
- Assumptions: how to state one, and separating them visibly from facts
- For each assumption, what breaks if it is false
- Which assumptions are load-bearing enough to verify before building anything
- New assumptions quietly introduced by a change — the most dangerous kind
- Why stating an assumption is a legitimate design output, not an admission of failure

## Guiding questions

- What limits you?
- What are you assuming without having verified it?
- For each assumption: what breaks if it is wrong?
- Which one should you go and check today?
